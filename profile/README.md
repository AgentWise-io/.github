# AgentWise

**The operating layer for global real estate.**

AgentWise is building infrastructure that connects professional identity, communication, property, buyer demand, permissions, relationships and outcomes into a permissioned market graph.

### Global Communications → Market Intelligence → Agentic AI

The product is designed around a simple progression:

**Professional Identity → Work → Trust → Market Memory → Intelligence**

AgentWise helps professionals work across markets while preserving ownership, source, permission, freshness and status.

### What we are building

- **Professional identity** that persists across a career
- **Global communications** for professional collaboration
- **Private Match** for permissioned supply and demand
- **Relay** to bring authorised context from the tools professionals already use into AgentWise
- **Market Memory** to preserve useful professional and market context
- **Agentic AI** as the future action layer built on top of that graph

> The models can be rented. The market graph has to be built.

### Product status

Community live · App in private beta · Private Match, Market Intelligence and Market Memory in development · Agentic AI and AI-to-AI are future direction.
