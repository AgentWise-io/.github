## What we're building

Real estate is one of the world's largest industries, but its professional activity is fragmented across independent firms, messaging applications, listing portals, local datasets, research and news.

AgentWise **started with the working professional community, before the app**. We now have an existing **Flutter/Django mobile app in private beta** and are assembling the engineers to take over delivery, ship V1, migrate professional activity from WhatsApp and start V2.

### The four layers

**01 — COMMUNICATIONS · V1**

A global professional operating layer: verified identities, a searchable People directory, dependable **one-to-one Chats**, professional **Rooms**, real opportunities and introductions. Messaging must work even without AI. Success means people keep working in AgentWise rather than returning to WhatsApp.

*Engineering:* Flutter/Dart; Django/REST; Channels/WebSockets; Redis/Celery; Firebase push; offline sync; group permissions; mobile release; analytics and retention.

**02 — RELAY · Planned**

Take one piece of work from where it happens and bring it into AgentWise deliberately: **Share → Relay → Review → AgentWise.** A selected message, property brief, PDF or voice note; extracted and structured with the user's approval. **Not** automatic access to entire private WhatsApp or email histories.

*Engineering:* iOS Share Extensions, Android sharesheet, parsing, multimodal AI, user review, provenance, privacy and secure local state.

**03 — MARKET MEMORY · In development**

**Historical evidence + licensed/global news and research + permissioned live professional activity.**

Property records and transaction history explain what *has happened*. Professionals reveal emerging buyer requirements, supply, changing availability and introductions *before* public records catch up. Connect this evidence into a global, source-aware market graph carrying **source, timestamp, freshness, permissions and confidence**. Longer-term institutional use includes lenders, developers, funds and research analysts.

*Mathematics and data:* entity resolution, knowledge graphs, geospatial data, recommendation systems, statistics, data quality, optimisation, graph databases, streaming/pipelines.

**04 — AGENTIC AI · Future direction**

An AI layer grounded in the authorised professional graph, not just a generic chatbot. Search intelligently, evaluate relevance, explain uncertainty, request holder approval, coordinate introductions and eventually complete supervised professional workflows.

*Engineering:* constrained matching, retrieval, model evaluation, tool/agent orchestration, authorization, governance and human review.

**Reference points:** The vertical AI depth of *Harvey*, intelligent messaging workflows associated with *Instinct*, and the data breadth of *Bloomberg/CoStar*. These are examples of ambition, **not partnerships**; advanced features are **not yet shipped**.
